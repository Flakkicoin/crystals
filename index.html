Crypto Academy · Beginner Friendly

New to crypto? Start here.

Six short lessons to take you from "what is a wallet?" to earning your first yield — explained without the jargon.

Lesson 01

What is a Token?

A token is a digital asset that lives on a blockchain. $CRYSTAL is an ERC-20 token — meaning it runs on Ethereum and can be sent, received, or swapped like any other currency.

  • Tokens have a fixed supply visible to everyone
  • Transfers are recorded on-chain, forever
  • You own them with a private key (your wallet)
Lesson 02

Setting Up a Wallet

A wallet is an app that holds your crypto. MetaMask, Trust Wallet and Rainbow are beginner-friendly. You don't "sign up" — you generate keys locally.

  • Never share your 12-word seed phrase
  • Bookmark official sites to avoid phishing
  • Start with a small test transaction first
Lesson 03

How Swaps Work

You don't buy $CRYSTAL from a person — you swap it on a DEX (decentralized exchange) like Uniswap. You trade ETH for $CRYSTAL directly from your wallet.

  • Always double-check the contract address
  • Set a slippage tolerance (0.5–2% is normal)
  • Gas fees vary — swap during off-peak hours
Lesson 04

What is Staking?

Staking means locking your tokens in a smart contract to help secure the network. In return, you earn rewards — like a savings account, but transparent and on-chain.

  • APY = annual % yield, what you earn in a year
  • You can usually unstake anytime (check terms)
  • Rewards compound if you re-stake them
Lesson 05

Reading a Chart

The price chart shows how much $CRYSTAL is worth over time. The line going up = more demand. Don't panic on dips — focus on long-term trends, not 5-minute candles.

  • Volume = how many tokens are trading
  • Higher timeframes = more reliable signals
  • Never trade with money you can't afford to lose
Lesson 06

Staying Safe

Crypto is full of opportunities and scammers. Stick to verified contracts, never connect your wallet to unknown sites, and use a hardware wallet for large holdings.

  • Revoke old approvals at revoke.cash
  • Enable 2FA on every exchange account
  • If it sounds too good to be true, it is
Safety & Glossary

Stay safe, speak the language

The crypto lingo demystified, plus the safety rules every holder should follow.

Verify Before You Swap

Always confirm the $CRYSTAL contract on Etherscan before any transaction. Bookmark the official site — never trust Google ads.

Guard Your Seed Phrase

Your 12-word recovery phrase is the master key. Store it offline, never type it into any website, and never share it — not even with "support".

Avoid "Free" Airdrops from Strangers

If someone DM'd you a surprise token, it's likely a scam designed to drain your wallet. Ignore and report.

DYOR — Do Your Own Research

No post, influencer, or admin can guarantee returns. Read the whitepaper, check the audit, and make your own call.

APYAnnual Percentage Yield — your yearly return, including compounding. ▶
If you stake 1,000 $CRYSTAL at 18% APY for one year, you end with 1,180 $CRYSTAL (compounded daily). The higher the APY, the higher the risk usually.
DEXDecentralized Exchange — a marketplace that runs on-chain with no middleman. ▶
Uniswap is the most popular DEX. You connect your wallet, pick a pair (ETH/$CRYSTAL), and swap directly. No sign-up, no KYC.
GasThe network fee you pay to miners to process your transaction. ▶
Paid in ETH on Ethereum. Higher gas = faster processing. You can set a lower gas fee and wait longer for confirmation.
LiquidityHow easily a token can be bought or sold without moving the price much. ▶
Locked liquidity means the project can't pull out the trading pool — protecting holders from "rug pulls". $CRYSTAL has 2 years of locked liquidity.
SlippageThe difference between expected and actual price when a swap executes. ▶
For small trades on liquid pairs, slippage is tiny (under 0.5%). For large trades or low-volume tokens, it can spike — set a tolerance to protect yourself.
WalletSoftware that stores your private keys and lets you sign transactions. ▶
Hot wallets (mobile/browser) are convenient. Cold wallets (Ledger, Trezor) are safer for large amounts since they stay offline.
HoldersThe number of unique addresses that own a token. ▶
A growing holder count is a sign of distribution and community health. $CRYSTAL's holder count grows daily as new users claim free spins.
StakingLocking tokens in a smart contract to earn passive rewards. ▶
Staked tokens still belong to you. You can usually unstake anytime. Rewards come from protocol fees and emission schedules.
